\subsection{Exact product-mixture representation}\label{cw:exact-product-mixture-representation}

For \(0<\beta\le1\), the Gaussian identity gives

\[
e^{\beta S^2/(2n)}=\sqrt{\frac n{2\pi\beta}}\int_{\mathbb R}
e^{-nz^2/(2\beta)+zS}\,dz.
\]

Thus \(\mu_{n,\beta}\) is exactly generated by:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Draw $Z$ with density \(\rho(z)=A^{-1}\exp(-nW_\beta(z))\), where \[
  W_\beta(z)=z^2/(2\beta)-\log\cosh z,
  \qquad A=\int_{\mathbb R} e^{-nW_\beta(z)}dz.
  \]
\item
  Given $Z=z$, draw all coordinates independently with common mean \(p=\tanh z\).
\end{enumerate}

Write \(\pi_p\) for this product law. The endpoint \(\beta=0\) is the uniform law and needs no mixture representation.
