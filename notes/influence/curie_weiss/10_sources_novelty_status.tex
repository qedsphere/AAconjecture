\subsection{Sources and novelty status}\label{cw:sources-and-novelty-status}

The derivations in Sections~\ref{cw:low-temperature-failure-for-every-fixed-beta1}--\ref{cw:consequence-exact-fixed-ux3b2-threshold} are supplied here, using the established uniform AA theorem and elementary inequalities. The Curie--Weiss transition and quartic law are classical. Relevant primary sources:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  R. S. Ellis and C. M. Newman, \emph{The statistics of Curie--Weiss models}, Journal of Statistical Physics 19 (1978), 149--161, \href{https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01012508}{DOI 10.1007/BF01012508}.
\item
  D. A. Levin, M. J. Luczak, Y. Peres, \emph{Glauber dynamics for the mean-field Ising model: cut-off, critical power law, and metastability}, \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.0790}{arXiv:0712.0790}. The proof above does not import its mixing-time results.
\item
  D. Turturean, \emph{A proof of the Aaronson--Ambainis conjecture}, \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09773}{arXiv:2611.09773}, for uniform AA.
\end{itemize}

No claim of novelty is established by an exhaustive literature review.
