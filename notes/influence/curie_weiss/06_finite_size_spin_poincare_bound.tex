\subsection{Finite-size spin Poincaré bound}\label{cw:finite-n-spin-poincaruxe9-bound-uniform-in-betale1}

For every real function $f$ on the cube,

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}_{\mu_{n,\beta}}f
\le(1+Cnb^2)\sum_i I_i^{\mu_{n,\beta}}(f)
\le C'n^{3/2}\max_i I_i^{\mu_{n,\beta}}(f).}
\]

Here \(0<\beta\le1\), while \(\beta=0\) satisfies the stronger ordinary product Poincaré inequality. To prove the displayed bound, write \(g(z)=\mathbb E_{\pi_p}f\) with \(p=\tanh z\). Conditional variance decomposition gives

\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\mu}f
=\mathbb E_Z\operatorname{Var}_{\pi_p}f+\operatorname{Var}_Zg.
\]

Product Efron--Stein says

\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\pi_p}f\le(1-p^2)\sum_i\mathbb E_{\pi_p}(D_if)^2.
\]

Differentiating the finite product-measure expectation gives

\[
g'(z)=(1-p^2)\sum_i\mathbb E_{\pi_p}D_if,
\qquad
|g'(z)|^2\le n\sum_i\mathbb E_{\pi_p}(D_if)^2.
\]

The second inequality uses Cauchy--Schwarz and Jensen. Integrate and apply Section~\ref{cw:elementary-latent-poincaruxe9-inequality} to conclude

\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\mu}f\le(1+Cnb^2)\sum_i I_i^{\mu}(f).
\]

Since \(nb^2\le\sqrt n\) and \(n\ge1\), the second bound follows.
