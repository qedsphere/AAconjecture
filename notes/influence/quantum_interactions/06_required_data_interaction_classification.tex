\subsection{Required data for an interaction
classification}\label{quant:sec-6}
\enlargethispage{\baselineskip}

A well-posed AA classification must retain:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Measured quantum thermal state versus diagonal-energy classical Gibbs
  law.
\item
  Measurement basis and coordinate/oracle encoding.
\item
  Graph family and dimension-dependent weight normalization.
\item
  Influence AA versus acceptance simulation, with temperature dependence
  of constants explicit.
\item
  Quantifiers: every allowed graph, one prescribed graph sequence, or
  some failing graph sequence.
\end{itemize}

Spectral information cannot replace the measurement basis. Graph
geometry and scale are likewise necessary.
