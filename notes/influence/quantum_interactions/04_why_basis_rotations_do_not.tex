\subsection{Why basis rotations do not preserve this AA
problem}\label{quant:sec-4}

In ground-energy estimation, conjugation by a known product unitary
preserves the task. Marwaha--Sud can therefore use such conjugations in
normal forms.

In a classical coordinate-query problem, the measurement outcomes and
their bit oracle are part of the task. Rotating the Hamiltonian while
retaining a fixed measurement basis changes the input law. The example
above proves this exactly.

If the measurement projectors are rotated as well, their outcome
probabilities are restored:

\[
\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[
e^{-\beta W^{\otimes n}\mathcal H W^{\otimes n}}
W^{\otimes n}|x\rangle\langle x|W^{\otimes n}
\right]/Z
=\langle x|e^{-\beta\mathcal H}|x\rangle/Z.
\]

That is a simultaneous change of physical representation, not a
classification of a fixed-basis classical oracle. Relabeling or
complementing classical coordinates preserves the query model; arbitrary
local unitaries need not.
