\subsection{Sources and status}\label{influence-recovery:sources-and-status}

Uniform AA is imported from the supplied exposition and Turturean's
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09773}{arXiv:2611.09773}, in the
normalization already used in this project. Product-bias continuity and
the slice-mean comparison correspond to Girish--Mittal--Nehoran--Raz,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35327}{arXiv:2609.35327}, Lemmas 4.6
and 4.9--4.10; the elementary proofs above make endpoint uniformity
explicit. The exact dyadic sampler avoids dependence on a target-bias
atom floor. The generalized-potential theorem and iteration are
deductions in this note. No research-priority or formal-verification
claim is made.
