\paragraph{Influence recovery: scope.}
The following notes derive generalized influence inequalities and the
corresponding restriction proofs from uniform-input AA. They apply to
every globally bounded low-degree polynomial, including polynomials not
known to be quantum acceptance probabilities. The exchangeable result
covers all magnetization-dependent Hamiltonians at every temperature,
temperature schedules, and limiting phase mixtures. Its extension to
\(r\) exchangeable species retains an explicit factor \(r^3\), allowing
correlated species magnetizations and fixed-species measurement laws.

The recoverable-source argument covers forests at every fixed finite
temperature, the stated bounded-degree high-temperature regime, and
independent local observation channels. Its constants depend on the
displayed temperature, degree, and contraction parameters. A short
forward sampler alone is insufficient: the proof also needs source
queries to be recoverable from the supplied physical input. These are
all-bounded-polynomial results. The separate arbitrary-degree
high-temperature theorem elsewhere in the dossier concerns quantum
acceptance simulation; the more restricted scope here does not withdraw
that theorem. No universal influence repair is asserted for Gibbs laws
that exhibit an actual query separation.

or computational-efficiency claim: preprocessing is unrestricted.
