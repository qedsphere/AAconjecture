The missing inequality is (S2), obtained from the atom-independent block
theorem (B2). The potential and restriction step are exactly (P1)--(P3),
and posterior access makes their source queries usable on the supplied
physical input. This is a derivation from Hamiltonian structure and
uniform AA, rather than the circular assertion that a short physical
transcript must explain variance because a simulator already exists.

The theorem is representation-dependent, as a collective influence must
be. A forward sampler with no locally accessible posterior is
insufficient. The independent sources in a genuinely hard character-code
distribution may contain a hard-to-recover code label, and no
posterior-query bound is supplied here for those negative families.
