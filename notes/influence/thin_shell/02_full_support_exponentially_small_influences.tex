\subsection{Full support with exponentially small
influences}\label{proof:thin_shell_obstruction:full-support-with-exponentially-small-influences}

Let \(\nu _n\) be uniform on \(\{x:S(x)\in\{-4,4\}\}\). Equivalently,
put probability one half on each uniform slice; they have equal
cardinalities. Define

\[
\mu_n=(1-e^{-n})\nu_n+e^{-n}U_n.
\tag{2.1}
\]

This law has full support and global spin-flip symmetry. Since \(f_n\)
is odd,

\[
\mathbb E_{\mu_n}f_n=0,\qquad
\operatorname{Var}_{\mu_n}(f_n)\ge(1-e^{-n})n^{-10}.
\tag{2.2}
\]

Equation (1.3) and \(|D_if_n|\le 1\) imply

\[
I_i^{\mu_n}(f_n)=e^{-n}I_i^{U_n}(f_n)\le e^{-n}\quad\text{for every }i.
\tag{2.3}
\]

\textbf{Theorem T1.} The full-support family (2.1) fails every
dimension-independent polynomial AA influence bound.

\textbf{Proof.} A bound \(\max _i I_i\ge c \operatorname{Var}(f)^A/d^B\)
with fixed \(c>0\) and finite \(A,B>0\) would, on taking \(d=5\), imply

\[
e^{-n}\ge\frac{c}{5^B}(1-e^{-n})^A n^{-10A}.
\]

This fails for all sufficiently large even \(n\) because exponential decay
beats every fixed inverse power. \qed

The variance tends to zero polynomially. Restricting a search to
counterexamples with variance bounded below by a constant would miss
this obstruction.
