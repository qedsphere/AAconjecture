\subsection{Implications ruled out and hypotheses
retained}\label{proof:thin_shell_obstruction:implications-ruled-out-and-hypotheses-retained}

Theorems T1--T2 disprove the following unrestricted implications to full
polynomial AA:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Bounded spin covariance norm implies AA.
\item
  Concentration of normalized magnetization near zero implies AA.
\item
  Absence of a distributed degree-one observable of constant variance
  suffices for AA.
\item
  Bounded Hamiltonian interaction arity suffices for AA at fixed
  temperature.
\end{itemize}

They do not refute a theorem additionally assuming uniformly bounded
incident interaction strength, a specified high-temperature condition,
uniform conditional atom lower bounds, or an appropriate low-degree
sampling map. The bounded-strength expander examples in
Appendix~\ref{proof:local_interactions} exhibit the different obstruction of
collective order at low temperature.

The issue concerns all globally bounded low-degree observables, not only
magnetization: (1.1) is exactly flat on every coordinate edge leaving
the typical shells while retaining inverse-polynomial variance between
them.
