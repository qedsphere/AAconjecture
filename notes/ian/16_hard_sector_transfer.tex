\subsection{Recognizable hard sectors and transfer}
\label{ian:hard-sector-transfer}

Status: proved, including an unconditional counterexample to full-AA transfer under a dimension-uniform KL bound.

We use the definitions of \(\ell_\mu\), \(\mathcal D_t\),
\(\mathcal F_t\), and \(\operatorname{Hard}\) from
Definition~\ref{ian:hard-sector-definition}. All trees have worst-case
query depth; preprocessing, computation, and real leaf constants are
unrestricted. Full AA has the quantifiers used in
Theorem~\ref{ian:hard-sector-aa-equivalence} and
Corollary~\ref{ian:hard-sector-resource-equivalence}.

\begin{corollary}[Recognizable hard sectors]
\label{ian:recognizable-hard-sectors}
In Theorem~\ref{ian:hard-sector-regularity}, the hard-set alternative
can be strengthened to require that \(H\) be a level set of a
finite-valued predictor computed by a deterministic tree of depth
at most \(Kt\), where
\[
 K=\left\lceil\frac1{w\gamma^2}\right\rceil.
\]
Consequently, for every law \(\mu\) on \(\Omega_n\), every
\(Q:\Omega_n\to[0,1]\), integer \(t\ge1\), and
\[
 0<\varepsilon<1/8,\qquad 0<\eta\le1/4,
\]
the assumption
\[
 \ell_\mu\left(
 \left\lceil256\varepsilon^{-7}\eta^{-3}\right\rceil t,Q
 \right)>\varepsilon
\]
yields a set \(H\) satisfying
\[
 \mu(H)\ge\frac{\eta\varepsilon^3}{16},\qquad
 \operatorname{Hard}\left(
 Q,\mu(\cdot\mid H);
 t,\frac{\varepsilon^2}{4},
 \frac{\eta\varepsilon^2}{4}\right),
\]
whose membership is decidable by a deterministic classical tree of
depth at most
\[
 \left\lceil256\varepsilon^{-7}\eta^{-3}\right\rceil t.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The proof of Theorem~\ref{ian:hard-sector-regularity} maintains a
predictor \(g\) that, after \(k\) updates, is a function of \(k\)
depth-\(t\) tests. It returns a hard set of the form
\[
 H=\{x:g(x)\in I_j\}
\]
before \(K\) updates have occurred. If
\(b(x)=j/M\) whenever \(g(x)\in I_j\), then \(b\) is computed by the
same tree and \(H=\{b=j/M\}\). Thus the returned set is a level set
of the binned predictor.

For the explicit conclusion, use the parameters in the proof of
Corollary~\ref{ian:hard-sector-explicit}:
\[
 a=\varepsilon^2/4,\qquad
 \gamma=\eta\varepsilon^2/4,\qquad
 M=\lceil1/\gamma\rceil,\qquad
 w=\varepsilon/(2M).
\]
That proof establishes
\[
 w\ge\eta\varepsilon^3/16,\qquad
 K\le\left\lceil256\varepsilon^{-7}\eta^{-3}\right\rceil.
\]
The preceding observation therefore supplies the stated membership
tree.
\end{proof}

For a Gibbs law
\[
 \mu_\beta(x)=\frac{e^{-\beta E(x)}}{Z(\beta)},\qquad \beta>0,
\]
write
\[
 Z_H(\beta)=\sum_{x\in H}e^{-\beta E(x)},\qquad
 F_H=-\beta^{-1}\log Z_H,\qquad F=-\beta^{-1}\log Z.
\]
The corollary gives a recognizable sector with
\[
 F_H-F
 =\beta^{-1}\log\frac1{\mu_\beta(H)}
 \le\beta^{-1}\log\frac{16}{\eta\varepsilon^3}.
\]
Recognizability here is a query statement with unrestricted
preprocessing. Its depth can be much larger than the depth \(t\)
against which the sector's acceptance signal is hard.

\begin{proposition}[Bounded-density transfer]
\label{ian:hard-sector-density-transfer}
Let \(\mu,\nu\) be laws on the same finite cube, and suppose
\(\nu\le K\mu\), where \(K\ge1\). For every \(Q:\Omega_n\to[0,1]\)
and integer \(t\ge0\),
\[
 \ell_\nu(t,Q)\le K\ell_\mu(t,Q).
\]
If a family \((\mu_n)\) has full-AA constants \(A,B\ge1\), and
\(\nu_n\le K\mu_n\) for every \(n\), then \((\nu_n)\) has simulation
depth
\[
 \left\lceil A(T+K/\varepsilon)^B\right\rceil
 \le
 \left\lceil AK^B(T+\varepsilon^{-1})^B\right\rceil.
\]
In particular, full AA transfers when \(K\) is bounded by a polynomial
in fixed model parameters alone.

If \(\sigma\) is a \(\nu\)-hard smooth sector of weight \(w\), then
the same hard law is a \(\mu\)-hard smooth sector of weight \(w/K\).
This also applies when the original sector is a set sector, although
its conditional law need not equal the conditional law induced by
\(\mu\) on that set.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For every \(D\in\mathcal D_t\),
\[
 \E_\nu|D-Q|\le K\E_\mu|D-Q|.
\]
Use a minimizing tree for \(\mu\). For full AA, simulate under
\(\mu_n\) to error \(\varepsilon/K\). Finally,
\[
 \nu\ge w\sigma,\qquad \nu\le K\mu
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \mu\ge(w/K)\sigma.
\]
The variance and covariance assertions concern \(\sigma\) itself and
therefore remain unchanged.
\end{proof}

A dimension-dependent estimate \(K_n=\operatorname{poly}(n)\) does
not supply the dimension-independent constants required by full AA.
The counterexample below already has
\(K_n\le\log_2 n\).

\begin{proposition}[Reconciliation with the repo's likelihood criteria]
\label{ian:hard-sector-likelihood-reconciliation}
Let the source law be \(\mu\), the target law be \(\nu\), and put
\(r=d\nu/d\mu\).

If \(p>1\), \(q=p/(p-1)\), and
\(\|r\|_{L^p(\mu)}\le K\), then
\[
 \ell_\nu(t,Q)\le K\ell_\mu(t,Q)^{1/q}.
\]
Thus a source simulation cost \(S(T,1/e)\) transfers to
\[
 S\bigl(T,(K/\varepsilon)^q\bigr).
\]
If instead, uniformly in dimension,
\[
 \E_\mu[r\mathbf1_{\{r>M\}}]\le CM^{-b}
 \qquad(M\ge1)
\]
for fixed \(C,b>0\), it suffices to use source error
\[
 e=\frac{\varepsilon}{2M},\qquad
 M=\max\{1,(2C/\varepsilon)^{1/b}\}.
\]

For a Gibbs law relative to the uniform law \(U_n\), let
\[
 W_n=\beta(\max E_n-\min E_n).
\]
Then
\[
 e^{-W_n}\le\frac{d\mu_{\beta,n}}{dU_n}\le e^{W_n}.
\]
A uniform-input simulator of cost \(S_0(T,1/e)\) consequently gives
acceptance simulation under \(\mu_{\beta,n}\) at cost
\[
 S_0(T,e^{W_n}/\varepsilon).
\]
In particular, \(\sup_nW_n<\infty\) preserves full AA, conditionally
on uniform-input AA.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For \(A=|D-Q|\in[0,1]\), H\"older's inequality gives
\[
 \E_\nu A
 \le K(\E_\mu A^q)^{1/q}
 \le K(\E_\mu A)^{1/q}.
\]
For the tail criterion,
\[
 \E_\nu A
 \le M\E_\mu A+\E_\mu[r\mathbf1_{\{r>M\}}]
 \le Me+CM^{-b}.
\]
The displayed choices make this at most \(\varepsilon\).

For the Gibbs assertion,
\[
 \frac{d\mu_{\beta,n}}{dU_n}(x)
 =\frac{e^{-\beta E_n(x)}}{\E_{U_n}e^{-\beta E_n}},
\]
and bounding the numerator and denominator by their extrema gives
the density bounds. Apply
Proposition~\ref{ian:hard-sector-density-transfer}.
\end{proof}

The \(L^p\) and tail assertions are exactly Proposition~\ref{tht:density-prop} in \path{foundations/transfers/03_density_transfer.tex}, with its source and target names interchanged; the Gibbs assertion is its bounded-oscillation special case. The bounded-energy subsection
\path{foundations/definitions/05_bounded_energy_sufficient_condition.tex},
under Section~\ref{note:model_definitions}, additionally uses the lower density
bound to transfer influence inequalities. Acceptance simulation needs
only the upper bound. Its dependence is polynomial in \(e^W\), rather
than necessarily polynomial in \(W\).

\begin{proposition}[KL conversion and its limitation]
\label{ian:hard-sector-kl-transfer}
Let \(\nu\ll\mu\), let \(g\) be real-valued on a finite space, and
let \(s>0\). Then
\[
 \E_\nu g
 \le\frac{D(\nu\|\mu)+\log\E_\mu e^{sg}}s.
\]
For \(0\le g\le1\), \(D(\nu\|\mu)\le C\), and
\(\E_\mu g\le\delta\), this implies
\[
 \E_\nu g
 \le\frac{C+\log(1+\delta(e^s-1))}{s}.
\]
In particular, source error
\[
 \delta\le\exp\left(-\frac{C+\log2}{\varepsilon}\right)
\]
suffices for target error \(\varepsilon\in(0,1)\).
Nevertheless, even \(D(\nu_n\|\mu_n)\le1\) for every \(n\) does not
preserve full AA.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Define the tilted law
\[
 \mu_s(x)=\frac{e^{sg(x)}\mu(x)}{\E_\mu e^{sg}}.
\]
Nonnegativity of relative entropy gives
\[
 0\le D(\nu\|\mu_s)
 =D(\nu\|\mu)-s\E_\nu g+\log\E_\mu e^{sg}.
\]
For \(g\in[0,1]\), convexity gives
\(e^{sg}\le1+g(e^s-1)\). Taking
\(s=(C+\log2)/\varepsilon\) proves the stated accuracy conversion.

For the counterexample, let \(m\ge4\) range over even integers,
\(n=2^m\), and use the character code \(C_n\) and the all-plus codeword \(c_0\) (the character with \(a=0\)) from
Section~\ref{qm:model-and-theorem}. Define
\[
 p_m=\frac1m,\qquad q_m=\frac1{\log m},
\]
where \(\log\) is natural, and set
\[
 \mu_n=(1-p_m)\delta_{c_0}+p_mU_{C_n},\qquad
 \nu_n=(1-q_m)\delta_{c_0}+q_mU_{C_n}.
\]
At other dimensions use the same point mass for both families.

The source family has full AA unconditionally, even for arbitrary
bounded targets. If \(\varepsilon\ge p_m\), output \(Q(c_0)\).
Otherwise query the \(m\) coordinates indexed by the standard basis
of \(\mathbb F_2^m\). On the support these determine the entire input,
so the output can be exact. In this case \(m<\varepsilon^{-1}\).

The log-sum inequality, applied to the common two-component mixture,
gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 D(\nu_n\|\mu_n)
 &\le q_m\log\frac{q_m}{p_m}
 +(1-q_m)\log\frac{1-q_m}{1-p_m}\\
 &\le q_m\log\frac1{p_m}=1,
 \end{aligned}
\]
because \(p_m\le q_m\le1\). Also
\[
 \frac{d\nu_n}{d\mu_n}\le\frac{q_m}{p_m}
 =\frac{m}{\log m}\le\log_2 n.
\]

For the repo's one-query acceptance function \(P\),
Section~\ref{qm:one-quantum-query-versus-logarithmically-many-classical-queries}
gives, whenever \(t\le m/2-2\),
\[
 \ell_{\nu_n}(t,P)
 \ge q_m\frac{1-2^{t-m/2}}2
 \ge\frac{3q_m}{8}.
\]
Take \(\varepsilon_m=q_m/8\). For every proposed full-AA constants
\(A,B\ge1\), all sufficiently large even \(m\) satisfy
\[
 \left\lceil A(1+8\log m)^B\right\rceil\le m/2-2.
\]
Thus the proposed depth has loss exceeding \(\varepsilon_m\).
The target family fails full AA.
\end{proof}
