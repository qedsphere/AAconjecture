\paragraph{Remarks.} (1) The repository uses ``full AA'' for acceptance simulation; Section~\ref{proof:positive_structure:two-sided-density-moments-and-antiferromagnetic-mean-field} also uses ``full polynomial AA'' for the influence statement. Here ``full AA'' always means simulation. (2) \texttt{papers/README.md} describes the Turturean theorem as the uniform bound every positive result imports, but the degree-free theorem and the quantum corollary appear to route through the product-alphabet completion and HMP instead. The dependency column records what the proofs cite. (3) The uniform theorem is cited as Turturean, arXiv:2611.09773, Theorem 1.1; it is not held in \texttt{papers/} and was not checked for this map.
