Status: a map, not new mathematics. Compiled from the repository at commit \texttt{116d91b} by reading statements and status lines; proofs were not re-verified here. Labels and file paths were rechecked after the reorganization in \texttt{fe1cd6f}. ``UAA'' marks results that import the uniform-cube influence theorem (Section~\ref{ht:conventions-and-imported-uniform-theorem}); ``Sim(U)'' marks results that assume uniform-input acceptance simulation instead; ``HMP'' marks the Boolean-box inequality of Huffstutler--Miloschewsky--Podder (arXiv:2611.12795) used in Section~\ref{sec:resolution-product-alphabets}. Sim(U) is now supplied by Corollary~\ref{cor:resolution-product-alphabet-aa} applied to the uniform law, with \(s_U(T,e)=O(T^6e^{-15})\), so an entry ``Sim(U)\(\to\)HMP'' rests only on (BC). UAA is not discharged as imported: Corollary~\ref{ian:product-influence-aa-proved} proves the influence form for every finite product law with the weaker exponents \((12,12)\) in place of \((5/2,6)\). \paragraph{Remarks.} (1) The repository uses ``full AA'' for acceptance simulation; Section~\ref{proof:positive_structure:two-sided-density-moments-and-antiferromagnetic-mean-field} also uses ``full polynomial AA'' for the influence statement. Here ``full AA'' always means simulation. (2) \texttt{papers/README.md} describes the Turturean theorem as the uniform bound every positive result imports, but the degree-free theorem and the quantum corollary appear to route through the product-alphabet completion and HMP instead. The dependency column records what the proofs cite. (3) The uniform theorem is cited as Turturean, arXiv:2611.09773, Theorem 1.1; it is not held in \texttt{papers/} and was not checked for this map.
