\subsection{Curie--Weiss and character-code checks}
\label{ian:generalized-influence-checks}

Status: proved conditionally on (UAA) for the full Curie--Weiss
generalized-AA assertion. The magnetisation reveal calculation and
the character-code refutation are unconditional.

\begin{proposition}[Ordered Curie--Weiss]
\label{ian:cw-generalized-check}
Assuming (UAA), all positive-probability pinnings of all zero-field
Curie--Weiss laws satisfy \(\Gamma^{\mathrm{rand}}\), with the universal
constants of Theorem~\ref{ian:exchangeable-gamma}. For every fixed
\(\beta>1\), the zero-field family fails the Local alternative as a
standalone influence theorem.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Curie--Weiss laws are exchangeable, and their pinnings remain
exchangeable. Apply Theorem~\ref{ian:exchangeable-gamma}.

For the failure of a standalone Local theorem, use
\(M=n^{-1}\sum_i x_i\). The results in
\path{influence/curie_weiss/02_low_temperature_failure_above_criticality.tex},
Section~\ref{cw:low-temperature-failure-for-every-fixed-beta1}, give
\[
 \deg M=1,\qquad |M|\le1,\qquad
 I_i(M)=n^{-2},\qquad
 v_n:=\Var(M)\longrightarrow m_\beta^2>0.
\]
Hence, for example with \(v=m_\beta^2/2\), every proposed positive
dimension-independent Local lower bound fails for sufficiently large
\(n\).

The identities recorded in
\path{synthesis/threshold_theory/06_generalized_influence.tex},
Section~\ref{tht:generalized-influence}, give exactly
\[
 \E[M\mid X_i]=v_nX_i,\qquad
 \Var(\E[M\mid X_i])=v_n^2.
\]
For every nonempty \(S\) and every \(i\in S\), the tower property and
conditional Jensen imply
\[
 \Var(\E[M\mid X_S])
 \ge\Var(\E[M\mid X_i])=v_n^2.
\]
Thus the magnetisation witness has Global gain at least \(v^2\)
whenever its variance is at least \(v\), even for a single uniformly
random queried coordinate.

This is consistent with the physical-update obstruction in
Proposition~\ref{tht:local-update-obstruction} in the same file: observing
a coordinate and changing a bounded number of physical spins are
different operations.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Direct failure for the character-code law]
\label{ian:character-code-generalized-failure}
Fix any \(\beta\ge1\). The character-code family
\(\{\mu_{\beta,n}:n=2^m,\ m\ge2\text{ even}\}\) satisfies neither
\(\Gamma^{\mathrm{rand}}\) nor \(\Gamma^{\mathrm{set}}\).
The same is true of its pinning closure.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Use the notation and exact mixture identity from
\path{obstructions/character_code/02_exact_mixture_identity_transition_width.tex},
Section~\ref{qm:exact-mixture-identity-and-transition-width}:
\[
 \mu_{\beta,n}=(1-w)U_n+wU_{C_n},\qquad w\ge1/2
 \quad(\beta\ge1).
\]
Let \(P\) be the globally bounded quadratic witness from
\path{obstructions/character_code/04_globally_bounded_quadratic_witness.tex},
Section~\ref{qm:a-globally-bounded-quadratic-witness}, and set \(f=2P-1\).
The calculations there give
\[
 |f|\le1,\qquad \deg f\le2,
\]
\[
 \Var_{\mu_{\beta,n}}f
 =(1-1/n)\left[w+\frac{2(1-w)}n\right]\ge3/8,
 \qquad
 I_z^{\mu_{\beta,n}}(f)=\frac{4(1-1/n)}{n^2}.
 \tag{17}\label{ian:code-local-bound}
\]
We prove that bounded-size coordinate sets also have vanishing reveal
gain.

Fix any \(S\) of size \(s\). Introduce the latent mixture label
\(\Theta\), so that \(X\mid\Theta=0\) is uniform on the cube and
\(X\mid\Theta=1\) is uniform on the code. The two component means of
\(f\) are equal:
\[
 \E_{U_n}f=\E_{U_{C_n}}f=-n^{-1/2}.
\]
Writing \(G_\nu(f,S)=\Var_\nu(\E_\nu[f\mid X_S])\), the tower property
and total variance therefore give
\[
 \begin{split}
 G_{\mu_{\beta,n}}(f,S)
 &\le\Var\bigl(\E[f\mid X_S,\Theta]\bigr)\\
 &=(1-w)G_{U_n}(f,S)+wG_{U_{C_n}}(f,S).
 \end{split}
 \tag{18}\label{ian:code-component-reveal}
\]
Equality of the component means removes a possible between-component
variance term.

The uniform-cube variance calculation in
Section~\ref{qm:a-globally-bounded-quadratic-witness} of the witness
file gives
\[
 G_{U_n}(f,S)\le\Var_{U_n}f
 =\frac{2(1-1/n)}n\le\frac2n.
 \tag{19}\label{ian:code-uniform-reveal}
\]

For the code component, write \(X=c_a\) with \(a\) uniform in
\(\mathbb F_2^m\), and let \(F(a)=(-1)^{h(a)}\).
Then \(f(c_a)=-F(a)\). Revealing \(X_S\) conditions \(a\) onto a
nonempty affine subspace whose codimension \(r\) is at most \(s\).
The affine-subspace bound proved in
\path{obstructions/character_code/06_one_quantum_query_versus_logarithmically.tex},
Section~\ref{qm:one-quantum-query-versus-logarithmically-many-classical-queries},
states
\[
 \left|\E[F(a)\mid X_S]\right|
 \le2^{r-m/2}\le\frac{2^s}{\sqrt n}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
 G_{U_{C_n}}(f,S)
 \le\E_{U_{C_n}}\bigl(\E[f\mid X_S]\bigr)^2
 \le\frac{4^s}{n}.
 \tag{20}\label{ian:code-code-reveal}
\]
Combining
\eqref{ian:code-component-reveal}--\eqref{ian:code-code-reveal} yields
the bound, uniform over all fixed sets \(S\),
\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
 G_{\mu_{\beta,n}}(f,S)\le\frac{2+4^{|S|}}n.}
 \tag{21}\label{ian:code-reveal-upper}
\]

Now consider any proposed six positive generalized-AA constants.
Take
\[
 d=2,\qquad v_0=3/8,\qquad
 \ell_0=\left\lceil B_G(2/v_0)^{K_G}\right\rceil .
\]
Choose an allowed \(n=2^m\), with \(m\) even, so large that
\[
 n>\max\left\{
 \ell_0,\
 \frac{2}{\sqrt{c_L(v_0/2)^{C_L}}},\
 \frac{2+4^{\ell_0}}{c_G(v_0/2)^{C_G}}
 \right\}.
\]
Equation~\eqref{ian:code-local-bound} defeats the Local alternative,
while \eqref{ian:code-reveal-upper} defeats the Global alternative
for every \(S\) of size at most \(\ell_0\). It therefore also defeats
the average over uniformly random \(\ell_0\)-subsets. The variance
hypothesis remains valid by \eqref{ian:code-local-bound}.
This refutes both variants for every proposed choice of constants.

The pinning closure contains these unpinned laws, so the same
counterexamples refute both variants on that closure.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Pinning closure and consistency with the bridge]
\label{ian:character-code-pinning}
The unpinned character-code Gibbs family should not be assumed
pinning-closed. A pinning conditions the hidden character index on
linear equations and changes the mixture weight.

More explicitly, pin \(s\) coordinates to an assignment consistent
with the code, and let \(r\) be the rank of its linear equations on
\(a\). The residual law is a mixture of the uniform residual cube
and the pushforward of the uniform law on the corresponding affine
subspace of hidden indices, with code-component weight
\[
 \widetilde w
 =\frac{w2^{-r}}{(1-w)2^{-s}+w2^{-r}}.
\]
If the assignment is inconsistent with the code, the residual law
is uniform. Thus the class of all positive-probability pinnings is
the appropriate class for applying Theorem~\ref{ian:bridge}.

The one-query function \(P\) has no dimension-independent classical
simulation bound at error \(1/8\), by
Section~\ref{qm:one-quantum-query-versus-logarithmically-many-classical-queries}
of the query-separation file cited above. If its pinning closure
satisfied either generalized-AA hypothesis, the bridge would imply
such a simulation already on the unpinned members.
Proposition~\ref{ian:character-code-generalized-failure} verifies the
necessary failure directly, using the same quadratic witness.
\end{remark}
