\subsection{Curie--Weiss and character-code checks}
\label{ian:generalized-influence-checks}

Status: proved. The full Curie--Weiss generalized-AA assertion uses
Theorem~\ref{ian:exchangeable-gamma}, whose influence input is proved
in Subsection~\ref{ian:influence-form-uniform-aa}; an earlier version
assumed (UAA), which is no longer used. The magnetisation reveal
calculation and the character-code refutation do not use that input.

\begin{proposition}[Ordered Curie--Weiss]
\label{ian:cw-generalized-check}
All positive-probability pinnings of all zero-field
Curie--Weiss laws satisfy \(\Gamma^{\mathrm{rand}}\), with the universal
constants of Theorem~\ref{ian:exchangeable-gamma}. For every fixed
\(\beta>1\), the zero-field family fails the Local alternative as a
standalone influence theorem.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Curie--Weiss laws and their pinnings are exchangeable, so
Theorem~\ref{ian:exchangeable-gamma} applies.

For the failure of a standalone Local theorem, use
\(M=n^{-1}\sum_i x_i\). The results in
\path{influence/curie_weiss/02_low_temperature_failure_above_criticality.tex},
Section~\ref{cw:low-temperature-failure-for-every-fixed-beta1}, give
\[
 \deg M=1,\qquad |M|\le1,\qquad
 I_i(M)=n^{-2},\qquad
 v_n:=\Var(M)\longrightarrow m_\beta^2>0.
\]
Hence, with \(v=m_\beta^2/2\), every positive dimension-independent
Local lower bound fails for large \(n\).

The identities recorded in
\path{synthesis/threshold_theory/06_generalized_influence.tex},
Section~\ref{tht:generalized-influence}, give exactly
\(\E[M\mid X_i]=v_nX_i\), so \(G_\mu(M,\{i\})=v_n^2\) for the
Curie--Weiss law \(\mu\). For every \(S\ni i\), the tower property and
conditional Jensen give \(G_\mu(M,S)\ge G_\mu(M,\{i\})\). Thus the magnetisation witness has
Global gain at least \(v^2\) whenever its variance is at least \(v\),
already for one random coordinate. This is consistent with
Proposition~\ref{tht:local-update-obstruction} in the same file:
observing a coordinate and changing boundedly many physical spins are
different operations.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Direct failure for the character-code law]
\label{ian:character-code-generalized-failure}
Fix any \(\beta\ge1\). The character-code family
\(\{\mu_{\beta,n}:n=2^m,\ m\ge2\text{ even}\}\) satisfies neither
\(\Gamma^{\mathrm{rand}}\) nor \(\Gamma^{\mathrm{set}}\).
The same is true of its pinning closure.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Use the notation and exact mixture identity from
\path{obstructions/character_code/02_exact_mixture_identity_transition_width.tex},
Section~\ref{qm:exact-mixture-identity-and-transition-width}:
\[
 \mu_{\beta,n}=(1-w)U_n+wU_{C_n},\qquad w\ge1/2
 \quad(\beta\ge1).
\]
Let \(P\) be the globally bounded quadratic witness from
\path{obstructions/character_code/04_globally_bounded_quadratic_witness.tex},
Section~\ref{qm:a-globally-bounded-quadratic-witness}, and set \(f=2P-1\).
The calculations there give
\[
 |f|\le1,\qquad \deg f\le2,
\]
\[
 \Var_{\mu_{\beta,n}}f
 =(1-1/n)\left[w+\frac{2(1-w)}n\right]\ge3/8,
 \qquad
 I_z^{\mu_{\beta,n}}(f)=\frac{4(1-1/n)}{n^2}.
 \tag{17}\label{ian:code-local-bound}
\]
It remains to show that bounded-size sets have vanishing reveal gain.
Fix any \(S\) of size \(s\). Introduce the latent mixture label
\(\Theta\), so that \(X\mid\Theta=0\) is uniform on the cube and
\(X\mid\Theta=1\) is uniform on the code. The two component means of
\(f\) are equal:
\[
 \E_{U_n}f=\E_{U_{C_n}}f=-n^{-1/2}.
\]
Conditional Jensen (conditioning additionally on \(\Theta\)) and total
variance therefore give
\[
 \begin{split}
 G_{\mu_{\beta,n}}(f,S)
 &\le\Var\bigl(\E[f\mid X_S,\Theta]\bigr)\\
 &=(1-w)G_{U_n}(f,S)+wG_{U_{C_n}}(f,S).
 \end{split}
 \tag{18}\label{ian:code-component-reveal}
\]
Equality of the component means removes the between-component term.
The uniform-cube variance in
Section~\ref{qm:a-globally-bounded-quadratic-witness} gives
\[
 G_{U_n}(f,S)\le\Var_{U_n}f
 =\frac{2(1-1/n)}n\le\frac2n.
 \tag{19}\label{ian:code-uniform-reveal}
\]

For the code component, write \(X=c_a\) with \(a\) uniform in
\(\mathbb F_2^m\), and let \(F(a)=(-1)^{h(a)}\).
Then \(f(c_a)=-F(a)\), and revealing \(X_S\) conditions \(a\) onto a
nonempty affine subspace of codimension \(r\le s\). The affine-subspace
bound proved in
\path{obstructions/character_code/06_one_quantum_query_versus_logarithmically.tex},
Section~\ref{qm:one-quantum-query-versus-logarithmically-many-classical-queries},
states
\[
 \left|\E[F(a)\mid X_S]\right|
 \le2^{r-m/2}\le\frac{2^s}{\sqrt n}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
 G_{U_{C_n}}(f,S)
 \le\E_{U_{C_n}}\bigl(\E[f\mid X_S]\bigr)^2
 \le\frac{4^s}{n}.
 \tag{20}\label{ian:code-code-reveal}
\]
Combining
\eqref{ian:code-component-reveal}--\eqref{ian:code-code-reveal}, for
every fixed \(S\),
\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
 G_{\mu_{\beta,n}}(f,S)\le\frac{2+4^{|S|}}n.}
 \tag{21}\label{ian:code-reveal-upper}
\]

Now consider any proposed six positive generalized-AA constants.
Take
\[
 d=2,\qquad v_0=3/8,\qquad
 \ell_0=\left\lceil B_G(2/v_0)^{K_G}\right\rceil .
\]
Choose an allowed \(n=2^m\), with \(m\) even, so large that
\[
 n>\max\left\{
 \ell_0,\
 \frac{2}{\sqrt{c_L(v_0/2)^{C_L}}},\
 \frac{2+4^{\ell_0}}{c_G(v_0/2)^{C_G}}
 \right\}.
\]
Then \(\ell=\ell_0\), the variance hypothesis holds and the Local
alternative fails by \eqref{ian:code-local-bound}, and
\eqref{ian:code-reveal-upper} defeats the Global alternative for every
\(S\) with \(|S|\le\ell_0\), hence also on average over random
\(\ell_0\)-subsets. This refutes both variants for every choice of
constants, and the pinning closure contains these laws.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Pinning closure and consistency with the bridge]
\label{ian:character-code-pinning}
The unpinned character-code family is not pinning-closed: its members
live on \(n=2^m\) spins, and pinning one coordinate leaves \(2^m-1\)
coordinates, which is not of this form. A pinning also
conditions the hidden index \(a\) on linear equations and changes the
mixture weight. Pin \(s\) coordinates to an assignment consistent with
the code, and let \(r\) be the rank of its linear equations on \(a\).
The residual law mixes the uniform residual cube with the pushforward
of the uniform law on the corresponding affine subspace of hidden
indices, with code-component weight
\[
 \widetilde w
 =\frac{w2^{-r}}{(1-w)2^{-s}+w2^{-r}}.
\]
An assignment inconsistent with the code leaves the uniform residual
law. Theorem~\ref{ian:bridge} must therefore be applied to the class of
all positive-probability pinnings.

By
Section~\ref{qm:one-quantum-query-versus-logarithmically-many-classical-queries},
the one-query function \(P\) has no dimension-independent classical
simulation at error \(1/8\). Through the bridge, this already forces
both generalized-AA hypotheses to fail on the pinning closure;
Proposition~\ref{ian:character-code-generalized-failure} verifies the
failure directly, with the same witness and on the unpinned laws.
\end{remark}
