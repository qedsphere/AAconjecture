\section{Ian: a program for distributional AA under Gibbs laws}\label{ian:section}

Status: program statement and map (Subsections~\ref{ian:program}, \ref{ian:repo-map}); generalized influence: a proved bridge to hereditary full AA, a proof of \(\Gamma^{\mathrm{rand}}\) for all exchangeable laws uniformly under pinning, and the Curie--Weiss and character-code checks (Subsections~\ref{ian:generalized-influence-bridge}--\ref{ian:generalized-influence-checks}); spectral high temperature: hereditary one-query simulation on the whole class, hereditary full AA under a Frobenius budget for \(\|K\|_{\mathrm{op}}<1/2\) (Theorem~\ref{ian:frobenius-full-aa}, Corollary~\ref{ian:frobenius-full-aa-half}), exclusion of the Hadamard forrelation statistic, and linear query barriers for two sampling primitives, with full AA for the entire class, quenched SK included, still open (Subsections~\ref{ian:spectral-high-temperature}, \ref{ian:spectral-posterior-barriers}); the limits of spin-level generalized influence: neither variant is necessary for hereditary full AA, a sharp block-size boundary, failure on Ising forests, and partial weighted high-temperature results with the row-sum-only question open (Subsections~\ref{ian:gamma-set-products-and-necessity}--\ref{ian:gamma-set-high-temperature}); hard sectors: failure of full AA is characterized by hard sectors with explicit polynomial extraction, transfer results (unconditional under bounded energy oscillation, and under any fixed R\'enyi order above one) and a counterexample under a dimension-uniform KL bound, explicit sectors in the repository's counterexamples, and sampling and Glauber consequences, the literal common-exponent H1 form remaining open (Subsections~\ref{ian:hard-sector-definitions-forward}--\ref{ian:hard-sector-sampling}); phase labels: admissible component decompositions, the phase-label theorem (failure of full AA implies a hidden (label, transcript) explanation on a hard sector; automatic label-only explanation on a prescribed hard sector is refuted; no converse in terms of natural phases), and its thermal Hubbard--Stratonovich reading (Subsections~\ref{ian:phase-label-admissibility}--\ref{ian:phase-label-gibbs}); influence-form uniform AA: \(\max_iI_i^\mu(f)\ge c_\star\Var_\mu(f)^{12}/d^{12}\) with \(c_\star=9/(2^{61}\pi^{12})\) for the uniform law and every finite product law, uniformly under pinning (Theorem~\ref{ian:uniform-influence-aa-proved}, Corollary~\ref{ian:product-influence-aa-proved}), and the recomputed constants it gives in Subsections~\ref{ian:exchangeable-generalized-influence}, \ref{ian:generalized-influence-checks}, \ref{ian:gamma-set-blocks-and-forests} and~\ref{ian:gamma-set-high-temperature} (Subsections~\ref{ian:influence-form-uniform-aa}, \ref{ian:influence-form-consequences}). Both uniform-input statements are now supplied inside the repository: acceptance simulation by Corollary~\ref{cor:resolution-product-alphabet-aa} and the influence form, with exponents \((12,12)\) rather than the imported \((5/2,6)\), by Corollary~\ref{ian:product-influence-aa-proved}. No result of this section is conditional on uniform AA. The remaining literature imports are inequality (BC) of Huffstutler--Miloschewsky--Podder (arXiv:2611.12795); the covariance inequality of O'Donnell--Saks--Schramm--Servedio 2005, with finite-dimensional LP duality, Chebyshev polynomials and Markov's inequality (Subsection~\ref{ian:influence-form-uniform-aa}); Girish--Mittal--Nehoran--Raz (arXiv:2609.35327) Proposition~4.12(1) and Lemma~4.9 for the slice step (Subsections~\ref{ian:exchangeable-generalized-influence}, \ref{ian:influence-form-consequences}; not held in \texttt{papers/}); the Bakry--\'Emery Poincar\'e inequality, and the F\"uredi--Koml\'os/Bai--Yin limit \(\|J\|_{\mathrm{op}}\to2\) for placing quenched SK in the class (Subsection~\ref{ian:spectral-high-temperature}); and Ding--Lubetzky--Peres, Theorem~3 (Subsection~\ref{ian:hard-sector-sampling}). Each subsection carries its own status line. The subsections were drafted by Astra, with some shorter proofs, corollaries and remarks added while editing; every result was checked lemma by lemma before inclusion. This section is self-contained and is not yet included from \texttt{main.tex}.
