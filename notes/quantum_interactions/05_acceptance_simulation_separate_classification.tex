\subsection{Acceptance simulation is a separate
classification}\label{quant:sec-5}

Both members of this pair have polynomial classical acceptance
simulation: the \(X\) model is uniform, while the \(Z\) Curie--Weiss law
is exchangeable and is covered at every temperature by
Appendix~\ref{proof:exchangeable_queries}.

Thus this pair separates influence-AA properties. It must not be
described as a quantum query advantage. The nonlocal character-code
Hamiltonian gives a genuine acceptance-simulation threshold; that
construction depends on the placement of its codeword states relative to
the coordinate-query basis.

There is nevertheless an exact isospectral obstruction for acceptance
simulation if locality is unrestricted. The flat-Hadamard proof of
Section~\ref{quant:sec-2} applies to every diagonal
Hamiltonian. Apply it to the character-code Hamiltonian \(H_C\) from
Section~\ref{proof:query_models} and its conjugate
\(W^{\otimes n}H_CW^{\otimes n}\). They have identical spectra at every
dimension, while their measured thermal input laws respectively exhibit
the proved query-AA failure at \(\beta\ge1\) and remain uniform at every
temperature. These Hamiltonians are nonlocal, so this does not establish
a two-local query-classification obstruction.
