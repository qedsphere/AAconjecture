\subsection{The structural data behind a meaningful temperature scale}
\label{threshold_physics:scales}

Temperature acquires meaning only after interaction strengths and
locality have been fixed. For a classical Ising Hamiltonian
\[
 H(x)=-\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}x_ix_j-\sum_i h_ix_i,
 \qquad J_* =\max_i\sum_{j\ne i}|J_{ij}|,
\]
the dimensionless small-coupling parameter is \(\beta J_*\), not
\(\beta\) alone. One-site conditional distributions obey
\[
 c_{ij}:=\sup_{x_{-j}=y_{-j}}
 \|\mu(X_i\in\cdot\mid x_{-i})-
       \mu(X_i\in\cdot\mid y_{-i})\|_{\rm TV}
 \le\tanh(\beta|J_{ij}|).
\]
Indeed a change of spin \(j\) shifts the effective dimensionless field
at \(i\) by \(2\beta J_{ij}\), and the maximal difference of the two
Bernoulli probabilities is \(\tanh(\beta|J_{ij}|)\). Thus
\[
 \alpha:=\max_i\sum_j c_{ij}
 \le\max_i\sum_j\tanh(\beta|J_{ij}|)\le\beta J_*.
\]
The bound survives every coordinate pinning: edges to pinned spins
become fields. This is the useful feature of a Dobrushin condition for
an adaptive-query proof. It is a condition on the conditional laws met
after transcripts, rather than on unconditioned covariance alone.

More generally for
\(H(x)=-\sum_{A:2\le|A|\le k}J_A\prod_{i\in A}x_i
-\sum_i h_ix_i\), telescoping changes in the conditional field gives
\[
 \alpha\le
 \max_i\sum_{A\ni i}(|A|-1)\tanh(\beta|J_A|)
 \le \beta(k-1)\max_i\sum_{A\ni i}|J_A|.
\]
Pinning again does not increase this bound. These inequalities identify
a plausible sufficient temperature \emph{scale}; they do not by
themselves prove full AA.

For a Pauli Hamiltonian \(\mathcal H=\sum_A\lambda_AP_A\), record
\[
 k=\max_A|\supp(P_A)|,
 \qquad s=\max_i\sum_{A:i\in\supp(P_A)}|\lambda_A|.
\]
These parameters remain meaningful for normalized dense interactions.
The high-temperature scale \(\beta sk\) is supported by quantum Gibbs
structure theorems discussed below. For transverse Ising,
\(s\le J_*+\max_i(|h_i|+|\Gamma_i|)\) under this convention.
Including independent fields in \(s\) is a conservative convention;
independent-spin laws themselves satisfy full AA at every temperature.

A sum of many weak terms cannot be classified from the spectrum of one
term. The graph, per-site summed strengths, growth of interaction
neighborhoods, and measurement/oracle basis all enter. Likewise,
\(O(n)\) total energy oscillation does not force a threshold: the
collective quantum family above has an extensive energy scale and full
AA throughout its phase diagram.
