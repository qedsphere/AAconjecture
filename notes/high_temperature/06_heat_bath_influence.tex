\subsection{Heat-bath influence}\label{ht:heat-bath-influence}

Define \(H_i^\mu(f)=\mathbb E_\mu \operatorname{Var}(f|X_{-i})\). In model (CW), the conditional mean of \(X_i\) is \(\tanh(\beta S_{-i}/n)\), so

\[
H_i^\mu(f)
=\mathbb E_\mu\left[
\operatorname{sech}^2(\beta S_{-i}/n)(D_i f)^2\right].
\]

Consequently,

\[
\operatorname{sech}^2(\beta) I_i^\mu(f)
\le H_i^\mu(f)\le I_i^\mu(f).
\]

For \(0\le \beta\le 1\), HT5 therefore also holds for maximum heat-bath influence, after multiplying its absolute constant by \(\operatorname{sech}^2(1)\).
