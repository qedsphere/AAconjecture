\subsection{The Curie--Weiss reference model}

The zero-field, extensive mean-field Hamiltonian is
\[
 H_n(x)=-\frac{(\sum_i x_i)^2}{2n}.
\]
Up to an additive constant, it equals $-n^{-1}\sum_{i<j}x_ix_j$.
The coupling incident to a spin is bounded, although the graph is complete.
The influence chapters use this reference model to distinguish a sharp
physical-influence threshold from full acceptance simulation. Their
Curie--Weiss proofs alone do not classify arbitrary graphs or all local
Hamiltonians; the separate simulation and high-temperature chapters
establish results for those broader structural classes.
