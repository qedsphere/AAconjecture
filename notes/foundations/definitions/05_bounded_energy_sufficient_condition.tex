\subsection{A bounded-energy sufficient condition}

Let $W=\beta(\max H_n-\min H_n)$, let $U$ be uniform, and write $r=d\mu/dU$. Then $e^{-W}\le r\le e^W$, so
\[
 V_\mu(f)\le e^WV_U(f),\qquad I_i^\mu(f)\ge e^{-W}I_i^U(f).
\]
The uniform-AA bound $\max_i I_i^U\ge cV_U^{5/2}/d^6$ consequently implies
\[
 \max_i I_i^\mu(f)\ge c e^{-7W/2}\frac{V_\mu(f)^{5/2}}{d^6}.
\]
This is dimension independent when $W$ is bounded independently of $n$. It is sufficient, not necessary: critical Curie--Weiss has energy oscillation of order $n$.
