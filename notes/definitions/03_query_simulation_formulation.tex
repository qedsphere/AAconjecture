\subsection{Query-simulation formulation}

For a family $\mu_n$, acceptance simulation means that every $T$-query quantum algorithm has its acceptance probability approximated to additive error $\epsilon$ on at least a $1-\delta$ fraction of $\mu_n$ inputs using $\operatorname{poly}(T,1/\epsilon,1/\delta)$ classical queries, with a polynomial independent of $n$. Fixed model parameters may affect that polynomial. Computation time and the cost of sampling the Gibbs measure are not included in this query bound.

Uniform AA implies this property for uniform inputs. A correlated-measure influence inequality alone does not supply the restriction argument needed for simulation. Section~\ref{proof:query_models} gives an actual query separation under a different Gibbs family.
