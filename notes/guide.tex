\section*{Reading guide and organization}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Reading guide and organization}

The notes distinguish full acceptance simulation from inequalities for
physical spin influences. A counterexample to the latter is not by itself
a counterexample to the former. Source files are organized by their
mathematical direction:
\begin{description}
\item[\texttt{foundations/}] Definitions, posterior transfer, reveal
progress, density comparison, and completion for product alphabets.
\item[\texttt{influence/}] Physical-influence results and obstructions,
mean-field studies, and generalized influence recovery for correlated laws.
\item[\texttt{simulation/}] Positive full-AA families: independent
blocks, forests, exchangeable inputs, and local observation channels.
\item[\texttt{high\_temperature/}] The verified classical weighted
interaction criterion and its extension to measured noncommuting Gibbs
states.
\item[\texttt{obstructions/}] Actual quantum-versus-classical query
separations from global, noisy, and bounded-degree local code Hamiltonians.
\item[\texttt{synthesis/}] Temperature-set conclusions, physical
interpretation, literature, and audits of upstream claims.
\item[\texttt{upstream/}] The supplied task summary and proof companion,
retained as source material with their original claim-status distinctions.
\end{description}

The independently compilable file
\path{../weighted_interaction_full_proof.tex} collects the classical
weighted-interaction proof and its dependencies. Its eight source bodies
were compared against verified revision \texttt{c7aa877}; they match
apart from the declared internal cross-reference adjustment. Its
mathematics also matches the audited revision \texttt{5fb8f18}; the
intervening change was equation layout. The standalone file imports the
uniform-AA and finite Boolean-box results explicitly, and needs no local
\verb|\input| files. The noncommuting quantum extension remains in
\texttt{high\_temperature/quantum/}.

Compile this collection from the \texttt{notes/} directory. Build
outputs, numerical checks, and superseded report assemblies are kept
outside the notes tree; no PDF is part of this source organization.
