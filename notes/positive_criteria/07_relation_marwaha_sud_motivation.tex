\subsection{Relation to the Marwaha--Sud
motivation}\label{proof:positive_structure:relation-to-the-marwahasud-motivation}

The supplied \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13026}{Marwaha--Sud paper,
arXiv:2604.13026} classifies ground-state estimation for quantum
two-qubit interaction families; EPR tractability remains conjectural.
This does not directly classify classical input laws. For a non-diagonal
quantum Hamiltonian, one must specify a distribution, for example the
computational-basis probabilities
\(\mu_\beta(x)=\langle x|e^{-\beta H}|x\rangle/\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-\beta H})\). The
structural criteria above apply if that resulting law satisfies their
hypotheses. No implication from local Bell-state energy ordering to
distributional AA is asserted.

The proved positive principles are controlled exact local sampling with
conditional nondegeneracy, uniformly nondegenerate products of bounded
blocks, and quantitatively shrinking latent mixtures. The
counterexamples identify global dependence and support hypotheses that
cannot be discarded.
