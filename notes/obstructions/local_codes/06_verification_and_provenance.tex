\subsection{Verification and provenance}
\label{res:verification}

The orbit, dense-energy, sparsification, and auxiliary-access arguments
were derived in the obstruction workstream.  The main workstream
supplied the fourfold parity symmetrization; the shared-reference
extension removes fields while preserving its symmetry.  Independent
main-workstream review checked the orbit, pruning, and fourfold
arguments.  The dense moment proof was checked directly against
Lata\l a's Theorem 1, including its tensor norms, the random-color
factor 27, Gaussian-magnitude Jensen comparison, and the separate
$p\le n$ and $p>n$ estimates.

Finite exhaustive checks at $n=4,16$ verified the Fourier identity,
the Fourier-maximum energy bound, all three flattening norms, and
the sparse soundness inequalities for two sampled test lists.  The
fourfold gadget was enumerated on all eight visible assignments and
all sixteen auxiliary assignments at six temperatures from zero to
two; maximum floating-point discrepancy was below $2\cdot10^{-15}$.
These checks support the algebra and do not replace the all-size
proofs.  The shared-reference gadget was additionally enumerated for
both reference signs: its ground costs are exactly $-8j$ and $-4j$,
and its marginal coefficient is $K(\beta j)rxyz$ as stated.
Reproduction scripts and numerical outputs are kept outside
the shared LaTeX tree, in the workspace's
\texttt{analysis\_work/resolution\_obstructions/} directory.

A separate check of the main workstream's three-level nonmonotone
model found the endpoint classifications consistent: with energies
$0$ on $c_0$, $(\log n)/2$ on the other characters, and $n\log2$
off the code, its normalizer is
$1+(n-1)n^{-\beta/2}+(2^n-n)2^{-\beta n}$.  On $1\le\beta\le2$
it dominates the uniform character law with a weight tending to one
for $\beta<2$ and to $1/2$ at $\beta=2$.  For $\beta>2$ its mass
outside $c_0$ decays as a fixed inverse power of $n$, allowing the
constant-output and small-size-readout simulation argument; below
one, comparison with uniform input has exponential error.  This
check is not a monotonicity claim for any local family above.
