\subsection{A query lower bound from character-symmetric thermal order}
\label{res:orbit}

Let $m\ge2$ be even, $n=2^m$, and index spins by $z\in\mathbb F_2^m$.
Write $c_a(z)=(-1)^{a\cdot z}$ and $\mathcal C=\{c_a:a\in\mathbb F_2^m\}$.
Distinct codewords have Hamming distance $n/2$.  Put
\[
 h(a)=\sum_{j=1}^{m/2}a_{2j-1}a_{2j}\pmod2,\qquad
 P(x)=\sum_{a:h(a)=1}\widehat x(a)^2,\qquad
 \widehat x(a)=\frac1n\sum_zx_zc_a(z).
\]
Parseval gives $0\le P\le1$ everywhere.  One phase query, a Walsh
transform, and the decision $h(a)=1$ have acceptance probability $P$.

\begin{lemma}[Character-orbit separation]
\label{res:orbit-lemma}
Suppose $\mu$ is invariant under $x\mapsto x c_b$ for every $b$, and
\[
 \mu(G)\ge1-\delta,\qquad
 G=\{x:\operatorname{dist}(x,\mathcal C)<\epsilon n\},\qquad
 0<\epsilon<1/4.
\]
Then every classical tree of depth $t$ satisfies
\[
 \mathbb E_\mu|D-P|
 \ge(1-\delta)
 \left[\frac{1-2^{t-m/2}}2-4\epsilon\right].
 \tag{OR}
\]
If the input additionally contains auxiliary spins that, conditional on
$X$, are independently sampled from kernels each depending on at most
$k$ visible spins, then (OR) holds with $t$ replaced by
$\max\{1,k\}t$, even when every auxiliary spin is queryable.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Every $x\in G$ has a unique nearest character $c_{A(x)}$, since two
balls of radius $\epsilon n<n/4$ are disjoint.  Set
$\eta=x c_{A(x)}$.  Multiplication by $c_b$ sends $A$ to $A+b$ and
leaves $\eta$ unchanged.  Conditional on $G$, invariance therefore
makes $A$ uniform and independent of $\eta$.  Moreover,
\[
 \widehat\eta(0)>1-2\epsilon,\qquad
 \widehat x(A)=\widehat\eta(0),\qquad
 |P(x)-h(A)|\le1-\widehat\eta(0)^2\le4\epsilon.
\]
After $\eta$ is fixed, each visible-spin query gives one linear
equation in $A$.  Any depth-$t$ transcript conditions uniform $A$ on
an affine subspace of codimension at most $t$.  Every Walsh coefficient
of $(-1)^{h(A)}$ has magnitude $2^{-m/2}$, so its bias on such a
subspace is at most $2^{t-m/2}$.  The best real prediction of the binary
target $h(A)$ thus has average absolute error at least
$(1-2^{t-m/2})/2$.  Subtract $4\epsilon$, integrate over $\eta$, and
discard the nonnegative loss outside $G$ to obtain (OR).

For the auxiliary version, realize every conditional kernel using an
independent uniform random seed, independent of $X$.  Fix all seeds
and $\eta$, and give them to the simulator.  Each auxiliary answer is
then computable using at most $k$ visible-spin answers, hence at most
$k$ linear queries to $A$.  An all-spin depth-$t$ tree is simulated by
a clean-character tree of depth at most $\max\{1,k\}t$.  Applying the
same affine-subspace bound and averaging over the fixed seeds proves
the assertion.  Randomized trees are covered by fixing their private
seeds too.
\end{proof}

This lemma requires no independence of the visible noise $\eta$ and
no single-coordinate influence inequality.  Its only thermal input is
concentration in disjoint character neighbourhoods and exact character
symmetry.
