\section{Local Hamiltonians with genuine quantum query obstructions}
\label{res:obstructions}

The following deductions concern full classical acceptance simulation.
They establish a fixed three-local Hamiltonian with a positive
high-temperature region and a low-temperature query separation, then
construct bounded-degree three-local and pairwise examples.  In the
pairwise construction every auxiliary spin is part of the input oracle.
No research-priority claim is made.
