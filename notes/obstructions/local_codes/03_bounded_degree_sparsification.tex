\subsection{Bounded-degree three-local Hamiltonians with a full-AA separation}
\label{res:sparse}

We sparsify the character tests while preserving the energy penalty
needed for the query obstruction.  Only soundness at a fixed relative
distance is required; no claim of soundness down to a single error is
used.

\begin{lemma}[A sparse family of character tests]
\label{res:sparse-lemma}
For every $n=2^m$ with even $m\ge2$, there is a multiset $\mathcal E_n$
of at most $Cn$ tests $x_zx_wx_{z+w}$, where
\[
 \epsilon=1/32,\qquad C=2048,\qquad D=12C=24576,
\]
such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item each spin occurs in the raw index set $\{z,w,z+w\}$ of at most
      $D$ tests, and hence in at most $D$ reduced tests;
\item every character satisfies every test;
\item every $x$ with $\operatorname{dist}(x,\mathcal C)\ge n/32$
      violates at least $Cn/128=16n$ tests.
\end{enumerate}
Each reduced test is either a three-spin interaction on distinct
nonzero indices or the one-spin test $x_0$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Sample $M=Cn$ ordered pairs $(z,w)$ independently and uniformly.
For fixed $x$, its test-violation probability is
\[
 q_x=\frac{1-\sum_a\widehat x(a)^3}{2}.
\]
If $x$ is at least $\epsilon n$ from every character, the bound in the
preceding proof gives $q_x\ge\epsilon$.  Chernoff and a union bound
over all $2^n$ spin configurations show that, except with probability
at most
\[
 2^ne^{-\epsilon M/8}=e^{-(8-\log2)n}<1/4,
\]
every such input violates at least $\epsilon M/2=M/64$ sampled tests.

Let $d_u$ count sampled tests whose \emph{raw unique index set}
$\{z,w,z+w\}$ contains $u$.  For $u=0$ the occurrence probability is
$(3n-2)/n^2$; for $u\ne0$ it is $(3n-3)/n^2$.  Both are at most
$3/n$, so $d_u$ is a
binomial variable of mean at most $3C$.  The identity for a binomial
size-biased first moment and the elementary binomial tail bound give
\[
 \mathbb E[d_u\mathbf1_{d_u>D}]
 \le3C\left(\frac e4\right)^D.
\]
Delete every test incident to an index with $d_u>D$.  The number $R$
of deleted tests is at most $\sum_ud_u\mathbf1_{d_u>D}$, whence
\[
 \mathbb ER\le3M(e/4)^D<M/512.
\]
Markov gives $\Pr(R>M/128)<1/4$.  With positive probability both this
deletion bound and the simultaneous soundness event hold.  The
retained degree is at most $D$, and every far input still violates at
least $M/64-M/128=M/128$ retained tests.  Character satisfaction is
preserved by deletion.  Choosing one successful test list for each
$n$ proves the lemma.  This can be made a computable, nonuniform
family by exhaustive finite search; no efficient construction is
needed for the query theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded-degree three-local failure]
\label{res:sparse-theorem}
Let $\mathcal E_n$ be as above and
\[
 H_n^{(3)}(x)=-\kappa\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_n}\prod_{u\in e}x_u,
 \qquad\kappa>0.
\]
For every fixed $\beta\kappa>\log2/32$, its Gibbs inputs violate
full AA.  The maximum number of incident terms is $D$, every term
has strength $\kappa$, and the incident absolute strength is at most
$\kappa D$.  Equivalently, after normalizing incident strength to
one, failure holds at every fixed inverse temperature
$\beta>D\log2/32=768\log2$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Every character has energy $-\kappa|\mathcal E_n|$.  A far input
violates at least $16n$ tests and hence has energy at least $32\kappa n$
above this value.  Thus
\[
 \mu_{\beta,n}\{\operatorname{dist}(X,\mathcal C)\ge n/32\}
 \le\frac1n e^{(\log2-32\beta\kappa)n}.
\]
Character multiplication preserves each retained interaction.  Apply
Lemma~\ref{res:orbit-lemma} exactly as for the dense family.
\end{proof}

This establishes a local-interaction obstruction with uniformly
bounded graph degree and incident strength.  It is a graph-local
construction; no fixed-dimensional geometric embedding is asserted.
