\subsection{Macroscopic-observable
criterion}\label{proof:higher_order_obstructions:macroscopic-observable-criterion}

Let \(f_{n} = \sum_{a}w_{a}\varphi _{a}\), with
\(\sum_{a}|w_{a}| \le  1\), \(|\varphi _{a}| \le  1\), and
\(\operatorname{deg} \varphi _{a} \le  k\) for a fixed \(k\). If
\(\varphi _{a}\) depends on \(S_{a}\), put
\(\ell_n=\max_i\sum_{a:i\in S_a}|w_a|\). Then \(|f_{n}| \le  1\),
\(\operatorname{deg} f_{n} \le  k\), and
\(I_{i}(f_{n}) \le  \ell _{n}^{2}\). Thus \(\ell _{n} \to  0\) together
with variance bounded below disproves AA.

If \(\mu _{n} = t_{n}\nu _{n}+(1-t_{n})\omega _{n}\),
\(t_{n} \in  [\eta ,1-\eta ]\), and the component means of \(f_{n}\)
differ by at least \(\delta\), then
\(\operatorname{Var}_{\mu_n}(f_{n}) \ge  \eta (1-\eta )\delta ^{2}\).
This proves failure for coexistence detected by a low-degree observable
with vanishing coordinate load. Coexistence alone, without such a
witness, is not a proof.
