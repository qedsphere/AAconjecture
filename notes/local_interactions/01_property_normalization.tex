\subsection{The property and its
normalization}\label{proof:local_interactions:the-property-and-its-normalization}

Let \(J\) be a symmetric, zero-diagonal \(n\)-by-\(n\) interaction matrix and take
no external field. Write

\[
H_J(x)=-\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}x_ix_j=-\tfrac12x^TJx,
\qquad
\mu_{\beta,J}(x)\propto e^{-\beta H_J(x)}.
\]

We fix the interaction scale by

\[
\max_i\sum_{j\ne i}|J_{ij}|\le1.
\tag{N}
\]

For \(|f|\le 1\) on the whole cube and degree at most \(d\ge 1\), use
\(I_i^\mu (f)=\mathbb{E}_\mu (D_if)^{2}\) with
\(D_if=(f(x_{-i},1)-f(x_{-i},-1))/2\). A family has influence AA if
there are \(c,a,b>0\) independent of \(n,d,f\) such that
\(\max _i I_i^\mu (f)\ge c \operatorname{Var}_\mu (f)^a/d^b\). Constants
may depend on a fixed inverse temperature or fixed structural
parameters.

Under (N) every finite-temperature law has the dimension-independent
finite-energy bound

\[
\frac1{1+e^{2\beta}}\le\Pr(X_i=1\mid X_{-i})\le\frac{e^{2\beta}}{1+e^{2\beta}}.
\tag{FE}
\]

Thus the negative examples below have full support and uniformly
nondegenerate single-spin conditionals. Their failure does not come from
forbidding configurations.
