\subsection{What has and has not been
classified}\label{proof:local_interactions:what-has-and-has-not-been-classified}

Proved sufficient positive structure: uniformly bounded independent
components, with finite-temperature atom bounds, using
Appendix~\ref{proof:positive_structure}.

Proved sufficient negative structure: a distributed phase witness (L1);
in particular, ferromagnetic weighted expansion at temperatures
satisfying (LT), and all its spin-gauge transforms. The failure persists
under bounded degree, two-body interactions, row normalization, full
support, finite energy, and trivial ground-state optimization.

Proved limits on proposed classifiers: the same local term and \(\beta\)
can have opposite AA outcomes on different graph families; even exactly
isospectral rank-two normalized matrices can have opposite outcomes.

Not proved here: a complete necessary-and-sufficient classifier for all
pair interactions, a sharp critical \(\beta\) for arbitrary expanders,
an AA theorem for every Dobrushin-unique or spectrally independent Ising
model, or a corresponding local-Hamiltonian quantum-query separation.
None of these stronger conclusions follows just because an available
proof stops working.
