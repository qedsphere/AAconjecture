\subsection{A temperature set with two disjoint positive regions}
\label{sec:resolution-nonmonotone}

All statements here concern a fixed inverse temperature as the input
dimension grows. A ``threshold temperature'' is not a universal form
of the answer, even for extensive, temperature-independent classical
Hamiltonians.

\begin{theorem}[Exact nonmonotone full-AA temperature set]
Let $n=2^m$ with even $m$, and let $\mathcal C$ be the character code
of Section~\ref{res:orbit}, with $c_0$ the all-plus character. Define
\[
 H_n(x)=
 \begin{cases}
 0,&x=c_0,\\
 \tfrac12\log n,&x\in\mathcal C\setminus\{c_0\},\\
 n\log2,&x\notin\mathcal C.
 \end{cases}
\]
Assuming uniform-input AA, the Gibbs family satisfies full AA exactly
at fixed inverse temperatures
\[
 \boxed{\quad\beta\in[0,1)\ \cup\ (2,\infty].\quad}
\]
It fails at every fixed $1\le\beta\le2$. The endpoint $\infty$
denotes the canonical ground-state limit.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The unnormalized partition function is exactly
\[
 Z_n=1+(n-1)n^{-\beta/2}+(2^n-n)2^{-\beta n}.
 \tag{NT}
\]
For $0\le\beta<1$, the mass on $\mathcal C$ is at most
$C_\beta n\,2^{-(1-\beta)n}$, and the conditional law off the code
is uniform. Hence
\[
 \|\mu_{\beta,n}-U_n\|_{\rm TV}
 \le C_\beta n\,2^{-(1-\beta)n}+n2^{-n}.
\]
This is bounded by $C'_\beta/n$. At target mean error $e$, if this
bound is at most $e/2$, use the uniform-AA simulator with error $e/2$;
otherwise $n\le2C'_\beta/e$, and querying the whole input suffices.
This proves full AA with constants allowed to depend on fixed $\beta$.

For $1\le\beta\le2$, the measure dominates a uniform-code
submeasure:
\[
 \mu_{\beta,n}\ge w_n U_{\mathcal C},\qquad
 w_n=\frac{n^{1-\beta/2}}{Z_n}.
\]
Indeed, every codeword has unnormalized mass at least $n^{-\beta/2}$.
Equation (NT) gives $w_n\to1$ for $1\le\beta<2$ and $w_n\to1/2$
at $\beta=2$. For the one-query acceptance $P$ of
Section~\ref{res:orbit}, every depth-$t$ tree therefore has loss at
least
\[
 w_n\frac{1-2^{t-m/2}}2.
\]
For $t\le m/2-2$ this is at least $3w_n/8$, so constant accuracy
requires $\Omega(\log n)$ classical queries. Full AA fails, including
at both endpoints.

For $\beta>2$, put $\gamma=\beta/2-1>0$. The mass outside $c_0$
satisfies
\[
 1-\mu_{\beta,n}(c_0)
 \le n^{-\gamma}+2^{-(\beta-1)n}
 \le C_\beta n^{-\gamma}.
\]
If this bound is at most $e$, the constant answer $P'(c_0)$
approximates any acceptance function $P'\in[0,1]$ with mean error
at most $e$, without querying the input. Otherwise
$n\le(C_\beta/e)^{1/\gamma}$ and full input inspection again has
polynomial query cost. At $\beta=\infty$ the law is exactly
$\delta_{c_0}$.
\end{proof}

The mechanism is explicit: an exponentially numerous bulk dominates
at high temperature, a hidden character label dominates at intermediate
temperature, and one known ground state dominates at low temperature.
The Hamiltonian has energy range $n\log2$, but has global interactions;
this example does not establish reentrance within a bounded-locality
class. It does establish that no monotonicity in temperature may be
assumed in a general classification.
