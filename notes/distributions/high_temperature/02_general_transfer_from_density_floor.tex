\subsection{General transfer from a density floor and an Lp bound}\label{ht:general-transfer-from-a-density-floor-and-an-lp-bound}

\textbf{Theorem HT1.} Let \(\mu_n\) have density \(r_n=\frac{d\mu_n}{dU_n}\). Suppose, uniformly in \(n\),

\[
 r_n\ge\ell>0,\qquad \|r_n\|_{L^p(U_n)}\le M<\infty
 \quad\text{for some }p>1.
\]

Set \(q=p/(p-1)\). Then every admissible \(f\) satisfies

\[
 \max_i I_i^{\mu_n}(f)
 \ge c_{\rm AA}\ell M^{-5q/2}
       4^{-\frac52(q-1)}
       \frac{\operatorname{Var}_{\mu_n}(f)^{5q/2}}{d^6}.
 \tag{HT1}
\]

\textbf{Proof.} Let \(a=\mathbb E_U f\), \(v_U=\operatorname{Var}_U f\), and \(v=\operatorname{Var}_\mu f\). Since \(|f-a|\le 2\), Hölder gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
v&\le \mathbb E_U[r(f-a)^2]\\
 &\le M\big(\mathbb E_U |f-a|^{2q}\big)^{1/q}\\
 &\le M\,4^{1-1/q}v_U^{1/q}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus \(v_U\ge M^{-q}4^{1-q}v^q\). Also \(I_i^\mu\ge \ell I_i^U\) for every coordinate. Applying (UAA) proves the claim. No independence of \(\mu\) is required. \(\square\)

This theorem is a sufficient condition, not a necessary criterion. In particular, its hypothesis fails at critical Curie--Weiss temperature even though AA still holds there, as proved below.
