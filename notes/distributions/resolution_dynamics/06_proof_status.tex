\subsection{Dependencies and verification status}
\label{resolution-dynamics:status}

The sufficient condition in
Theorem~\ref{thm:resolution-dynamics-arbitrary-degree} is proved by
three explicit reductions: the parity-map estimates in
Section~\ref{resolution-dynamics:weighted-maps}, product-alphabet
completion in Section~\ref{sec:resolution-product-alphabets}, and
the conditional history construction in
Section~\ref{resolution-dynamics:arbitrary-degree}.
The completion step imports the finite Boolean-box inequality stated
there from the supplied completion-framework paper. The measured
noncommuting extension additionally imports the abstract-polymer
bound stated in Section~\ref{quantum-clusters:effective}.

The coordinating workstream and separate theory workstreams reviewed
the map probabilities, field-uniform posterior bound, weighted-box
counting, and the distinction between complete-history conditioning
and the lazy sampler's exposed-information filtration. Small-system
checks support the earlier finite-history identities; they are not
proofs of the dimension-uniform statements. Reproduction scripts and
outputs are outside the repository's notes tree.

\textbf{Post-push verification, 14 November 2026.}
The full proof was first pushed as
\href{https://github.com/qedsphere/AAconjecture/commit/5fb8f1880dba0bb8798a0a015a82759f5bd5fb1f}
{commit \texttt{5fb8f18}}. Three theory workstreams then audited that
exact revision. The dynamics proof was checked by the obstruction and
structural workstreams; the quantum proof by the dynamics and structural
workstreams. The coordinating workstream also checked both chains.
No substantive mathematical gap or correction was identified.

The checks covered conditional dependency paths and truncation,
whole-symbol versus physical-spin query accounting, the two distinct
filtrations in the adaptive posterior proof, and the final error and
query exponents. On the quantum side they covered repeated term labels,
noncommuting word interleavings, the connected-tree count, countable
self-incompatible polymers, the real logarithm, Fourier weights, POVM
zero effects, and pinnings. The pinned convergence bound was checked
again directly against Fern\'andez--Procacci, Section 2, equations
(2.7) and (2.12)--(2.15). The product-alphabet step was checked against
the supplied completion paper's finite Boolean-box inequality.

Finite checks supplemented this audit. Across 160 biased ternary-product
cases, 4,320 linear-programming certificates and 34,560 two-point boxes
passed; the pair and edge identities differed by at most
$2.3\cdot10^{-16}$ in floating point. Seventy-five local parity-map
cases, with fields ranging from $-40$ to $40$ and arithmetic precision
85 decimal digits, passed the support-over-output-probability bound;
the largest observed ratio was $0.678<160/169$, including constant-map
probabilities below $1.5\cdot10^{-35}$.

Independent noncommuting polymer enumerations through orders four and
five agreed with matrix Taylor coefficients within
$2.3\cdot10^{-16}$. Exact matrix-exponential calculations for 24
four-qubit models satisfied the effective interaction bound and its
pinning monotonicity. Random seeds were 46183 and 46184 for the
structural workstream and 515818 for the coordinating checks.
An isolated export of the pushed source compiled successfully; build
and numerical artifacts stayed outside the repository.

These are mathematical reviews and finite checks, not proof-assistant
certification. The sufficient constants and query exponents are not
claimed sharp. No computational-time bound, state-preparation bound,
or theorem for collective measurements is asserted.
