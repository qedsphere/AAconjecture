\subsection{Uniform low-degree moment comparison}\label{cw:uniform-low-degree-moment-comparison}

For a multilinear polynomial $h$ of degree at most $k$ with \(|h|\le1\) on the entire cube, \(q(p)=\mathbb E_{\pi_p}h\) is a univariate polynomial of degree at most $k$ satisfying \(|q|\le1\) on \([-1,1]\). Applying the ordinary Markov polynomial inequality twice gives \(\lVert q^{\prime\prime}\rVert_\infty\le k^4\). Since \(\mathbb E P=0\), Taylor's formula gives

\[
|\mathbb E_\mu h-\mathbb E_Uh|
=|\mathbb E q(P)-q(0)|
\le\tfrac12 k^4\mathbb E P^2
\le Ck^4 n^{-1/2}.
\]

Apply this to $f$ and to the multilinearization of \(f^2\). If \(|f|\le1\) and \(\deg f\le d\), then

\[
\boxed{|\operatorname{Var}_{\mu}f-\operatorname{Var}_Uf|
\le Cd^4n^{-1/2}.}
\]

The central density bound from Section~\ref{cw:uniform-latent-estimates-throughout-0betale1}, applied to the nonnegative polynomial \(p\mapsto\mathbb E_{\pi_p}(D_i f)^2\), also yields a multiplicative comparison \(I_i^\mu\ge c I_i^U/d^2\). The polynomial integral lemma and the resulting combination are proved in Section~\ref{proof:high_temperature}.
