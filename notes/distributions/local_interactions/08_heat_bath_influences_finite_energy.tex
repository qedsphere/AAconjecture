\subsection{Heat-bath influences and finite
energy}\label{proof:local_interactions:heat-bath-influences-and-finite-energy}

Let \(B_i^\mu (f)=\mathbb{E}_\mu  \operatorname{Var}(f|X_{-i})\). For
the normalized zero-field pair model,

\[
B_i^\mu(f)=\mathbb{E}_\mu\left[\operatorname{sech}^2\!\left(\beta\sum_jJ_{ij}X_j\right)(D_if)^2\right].
\]

Consequently

\[
\operatorname{sech}^2(\beta)I_i^\mu(f)\le B_i^\mu(f)\le I_i^\mu(f).
\]

The matching positive theorem and all the fixed-\(\beta\) negative
examples therefore hold for both influence conventions. In particular, a
uniformly positive conditional resampling probability is insufficient
for AA.
