\subsection{Weighted expansion gives a genuine low-temperature
counterexample}\label{proof:local_interactions:weighted-expansion-gives-a-genuine-low-temperature-counterexample}

Let \(J_{ij}\ge 0\). For \(S\subseteq [n]\), put

\[
B_J(S)=\sum_{i\in S,\,j\notin S}J_{ij}.
\]

Assume there is a fixed \(\kappa >0\) such that

\[
B_J(S)\ge\kappa |S|\qquad(1\le |S|\le n/2).
\tag{E}
\]

\textbf{Theorem L2.} Fix \(\alpha \in (0,1/2)\), and suppose

\[
2\beta\kappa\alpha>\log2.
\tag{LT}
\]

For every ferromagnetic family satisfying (E), the magnetization
\(M=n^{-1}\sum_iX_i\) obeys

\[
\Pr_{\mu_{\beta,J}}(|M|\le1-2\alpha)
\le\tfrac12 e^{-n(2\beta\kappa\alpha-\log2)},
\tag{3.1}
\]

and hence

\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\mu_{\beta,J}}(M)
\ge(1-2\alpha)^2\left[1-\tfrac12e^{-n(2\beta\kappa\alpha-\log2)}\right].
\tag{3.2}
\]

All normalized flip influences of M equal \(n^{-2}\). Therefore
influence AA fails. The sufficient temperature condition is explicit but
is not asserted to be the sharp critical temperature of the graph
family.

\textbf{Proof.} Let \(S=\{i:x_i=-1\}\). Relative to the all-plus
configuration,

\[
H_J(x)-H_J(\mathbf1)=2B_J(S).
\]

If \(|M(x)|\le 1-2\alpha\), then both \(|S|\) and \(|S^{c}|\) are at
least \(\alpha n\). Apply (E) to whichever is smaller to obtain
\(B_J(S)\ge \kappa \alpha n\). Every configuration in this event
therefore has Gibbs weight at most \(\exp (-2\beta \kappa \alpha n)\)
times the all-plus weight. There are at most \(2^n\) such
configurations, while the partition function is at least twice the
all-plus weight because the all-minus configuration has the same energy.
This proves (3.1). Global spin-flip symmetry gives \(\mathbb{E} M=0\),
and outside the event \(M^{2}>(1-2\alpha )^{2}\), proving (3.2). Finally
\(D_iM=1/n\). \qed

This elementary entropy--energy comparison proves a counterexample. It
does not merely observe that a high-temperature proof stops applying.

It is specifically an influence counterexample. The witness M itself is
easy to approximate classically: sampling
\(O(\epsilon ^{-2}\log (1/\delta ))\) uniformly chosen coordinates
estimates its value within \(\epsilon\) with failure probability at most
\(\delta\), for every fixed input string. The same observation applies
to staggered magnetization. Thus this proof supplies no
quantum-versus-classical query separation for the local Gibbs families.
