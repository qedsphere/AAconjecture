\subsection{The physical model resolved in this dossier}

The zero-field, extensive mean-field Hamiltonian is
\[
 H_n(x)=-\frac{(\sum_i x_i)^2}{2n}.
\]
Up to an additive constant, it equals $-n^{-1}\sum_{i<j}x_ix_j$. The coupling incident to a spin is bounded, although the graph is complete. These proofs do not classify arbitrary graphs, external fields, all local Hamiltonians, or every dimension-dependent temperature schedule.
