\section{Measured noncommuting Hamiltonians at high temperature}
\label{res:quantum-clusters}

This section proves a reduction from a noncommuting local Hamiltonian to
a classical interaction with small weighted Fourier norm.  Its conclusion
is stronger than decay of two-point correlations: it bounds the absolute
sum of every effective interaction incident to any one measured spin.
The estimate has no interaction-degree factor and holds for arbitrary
binary product measurements.  The only imported analytic ingredient is
the abstract-polymer cluster expansion, stated explicitly below.
