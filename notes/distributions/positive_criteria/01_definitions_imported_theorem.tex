\subsection{Definitions and imported
theorem}\label{proof:positive_structure:definitions-and-imported-theorem}

For a law \(\mu\) on \(\{-1,1\}^n\), a multilinear polynomial \(f\)
bounded by one on the entire cube, and \(d\ge \operatorname{deg}(f)\),
set

\[
D_if(x_{-i})=\frac{f(x_{-i},1)-f(x_{-i},-1)}2,
\quad I_i^\mu(f)=\mathbb{E}_\mu(D_if)^2,
\quad v=\operatorname{Var}_\mu f.
\]

A family has polynomial AA if \(\max _i I_i^\mu (f)\ge c v^A/d^B\), with
finite constants independent of dimension, degree, and polynomial.
Dependence on explicitly fixed structural parameters is allowed and
recorded.

We import the uniform-cube theorem

\[
\max_j I_j^{U_N}(F)\ge c_0\operatorname{Var}_{U_N}(F)^{5/2}/D^6
\tag{UAA}
\]

for globally bounded degree-\(D\) functions. See
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09773}{Turturean, Theorem 1.1}. The
proofs below explain their reductions to this theorem.
