\subsection{A fixed sample estimates the remaining
density}\label{exch:sec-4}

Fix total weight \(k\) and \(X\sim U_{n,k}\). Query the first \(L\)
coordinates, assuming \(n\ge2L\). Let \(Y\) be their number of ones,
\(q=Y/L\), \(N=n-L\), and \(p=(k-Y)/N\).

Given the complete observed prefix, the remaining input is uniform on
the slice of weight \(k-Y\). Hypergeometric sampling gives

\[
\mathbb E q=k/n,\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(q)=\frac{(k/n)(1-k/n)}L\,\frac{n-L}{n-1}\le\frac1{4L}.
\]

Since \(p-q=\frac{n}{n-L}(k/n-q)\) and \(n/(n-L)\le2\), Chebyshev's
inequality yields

\[
\Pr[|p-q|>\eta]\le1/(L\eta^2).
\]

This holds for every \(k\), without randomizing the queried locations.
The final simulator is therefore deterministic and independent of the
unknown slice or mixture.
