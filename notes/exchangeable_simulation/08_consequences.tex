\subsection{Consequences}\label{exch:heading-9}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Every classical Gibbs Hamiltonian depending only on total
  magnetization is covered, with arbitrary dimension-dependent
  temperature or energy normalization.
\item
  Curie--Weiss has no failure temperature for polynomial classical
  acceptance simulation. Its influence threshold classifies a different
  property.
\item
  Exchangeable phase coexistence can produce small local sensitivities
  while remaining classically simulable in the AA query sense.
\item
  The character-code Gibbs measure in
  Section~\ref{proof:query_models} is not exchangeable; its
  genuine query-AA threshold is consistent with this theorem.
\item
  The theorem concerns designated acceptance probabilities, not search
  or sampling outputs.
\end{enumerate}
